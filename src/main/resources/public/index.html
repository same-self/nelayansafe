    Conservative System Extensions (Decision Rules)

    Applied uniformly on top of official BMKG thresholds to prioritize crew safety before departure.

    DO NOT SAIL Conservative OR Gate

    Triggered when windSpeed ≥ maxSafeWind OR waveHeight ≥ maxSafeWave. Inclusive limits prevent sailing right at BMKG danger boundaries.

    CAUTION 75% Buffer, Gust and Current Rules

    Triggered when wave or wind reaches ≥ 75% of the vessel limit, windGust ≥ maxSafeWind, currentSpeed ≥ 2.0 kt, or a fishing boat's tighter current limit.

    0 to 100 SCALE Polymorphic Risk Score Formula

    min(100, round(85 * max(wind/maxWind, wave/maxWave) + calculateVesselModifier(slot))), combining base BMKG severity (0 to 85 pts) with subclass exposure (0 to 15 pts).

    4 CHECKED EXCEPTIONS Pre-Departure Custom Exceptions

    VesselOvercapacityException (crew, cargo, or passengers), MissingSafetyGearException (life jackets or radio), UnsafeSeaConditionException (blocked weather or unacknowledged caution), and BmkgApiUnavailableException (offline with no cache).
