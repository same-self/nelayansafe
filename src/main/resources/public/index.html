    Official BMKG Vessel Sailing Safety Thresholds

    Every wind and wave limit is mapped directly to official BMKG Marine Meteorology guidelines.

    Java Vessel Subclass BMKG Vessel Class Risky Wind Limit Risky Wave Limit Subclass Attributes and Polymorphic Modifiers (0 to 15 pts)
    SmallFishingBoat Perahu Nelayan ≥ 15 knots ≥ 1.25 m grossTonnage, maxSafeCurrentKnots; wind gust excess, current drift, small-hull vulnerability (<10 GT)
    BargeVessel Kapal Tongkang ≥ 16 knots ≥ 1.50 m maxCargoTon, currentCargoTon; current drag, cross-gusts, cargo load ratio + cargo overcapacity gate
    PassengerFerry Kapal Ferry ≥ 21 knots ≥ 2.50 m maxPassengers, currentPassengers; swell roll stability, occupancy margin, gusts + passenger overcapacity gate
    CargoShip Kapal Kargo / Pesiar ≥ 27 knots ≥ 4.00 m maxContainerTEU; container stack windage (TEU) and high-wind gust exposure

    Conservative System Extensions (Decision Rules)

    Applied uniformly on top of official BMKG thresholds to prioritize crew safety before departure.

    • DO_NOT_SAIL (Conservative OR Gate): Triggered when windSpeed ≥ maxSafeWind OR waveHeight ≥ maxSafeWave (stricter than BMKG's combined advisory wording).
    • CAUTION Buffer: Triggered when wave or wind reaches ≥ 75% of the vessel limit, windGust ≥ maxSafeWind, or currentSpeed ≥ 2.0 kt.
    • Polymorphic Risk Score (0 to 100): min(100, round(85 * max(wind/maxWind, wave/maxWave) + calculateVesselModifier(slot))), where each subclass computes a 0 to 15 point modifier.
    • Pre-Departure Custom Exceptions: VesselOvercapacityException (crew, cargo tons, or passengers), MissingSafetyGearException, UnsafeSeaConditionException, and BmkgApiUnavailableException.
